Faculty Viva Defense & Evaluation Rubric Reference
Master answers for the 2-minute individual viva defense required by INT335 guidelines.
100 Marks Rubric
Q1: Why did your team not build an AI WhatsApp Chatbot?
Defense: In Stage 3 Ideation, our pre-weighted screening matrix rejected AI bots (Score: 3.01 vs ClubSync 4.78). Freshers reported that automated bots feel impersonal and increase spam; what they actually needed was psychological safety and a 1-to-1 senior mentor connection.
Q2: How does your POV statement prove you didn't guess?
Defense: Our POV focuses on lead delegation fear: "Aarav needs a low-risk way to delegate micro-tasks because open volunteer requests trigger bystander silence, forcing him to hoard work until 2:30 AM." Every clause traces to verbatim interview records in CP2.
Q3: What critical finding came from Stage 5 User Testing?
Defense: Testing revealed that students feared harsh judgment on starter drafts and hesitated over the term "Sabbatical". We iterated by renaming it to "Take an Exam Break" and adding the psychological safety reassurance banner, jumping completion from 20% to 80%.
Q4: Why does this beat doing nothing on campus?
Defense: Without intervention, 85% of recruits ghost by week 4 and club presidents burn out every semester. ClubSync delivers a 4x increase in active contributors and reduces solo lead workload by 30% with zero monetary overhead.
